# Hivra trademark policy

The Apache-2.0 license grants broad rights to the code. It does not grant a
license to the Hivra name, logos, product artwork, or other source-identifying
marks, except as needed for accurate attribution and nominative description.

Forks and competing hosted services are explicitly permitted. You may say that
your project is based on Hivra, compatible with Hivra, or a fork of Hivra. You
may reproduce the Hivra name where the software itself displays accurate
copyright, license, provenance, or compatibility information.

Unless you have separate written permission, do not:

- name a modified distribution or hosted service "Hivra";
- use Hivra logos as the primary identity of a fork or service;
- use a confusingly similar domain, package, organization, or application name;
- imply that Hivra endorses, operates, secures, or supports your distribution;
  or
- remove notices that make the origin and modification status clear.

Modified distributions should use their own name and visual identity, identify
material changes, and retain the required Apache-2.0 and third-party notices.
Unmodified redistribution may retain factual Hivra branding inside the software
provided the distributor and support owner are clearly identified.

This policy controls branding only. It must not be used to restrict the code
rights granted by Apache-2.0. Questions about permitted brand use can be sent to
info@hermesos.cloud.
